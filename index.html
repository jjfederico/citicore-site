The Firm

A vertically integrated operator. One decision-making team from first call to stabilized asset.

Most sellers deal with layers: an acquisitions desk, an outside investment committee, a third-party manager, a listing broker. Citicore removes them. We buy, build, manage and broker through our own platform, so the people who underwrite your building are the people who close it and operate it.

That structure is why we move faster than larger funds and underwrite more carefully than smaller buyers. We price on real operating data, commit to our numbers early and do not retrade on issues that were visible at the outset.
