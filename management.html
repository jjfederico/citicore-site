Services

What management covers

Leasing & rent roll
Tenant placement through our brokerage arm, renewals handled early, and rents reviewed against the market at every turn — the rent roll is managed, not just collected.
Collections & reporting
Rent collection, monthly owner statements, and lender-ready reporting. You see the same numbers we run the building on.
Maintenance & capital
Day-to-day maintenance through a proven vendor bench, plus a written capital plan for every building — roofs, systems and units sequenced before they become emergencies.
Compliance
City registrations, inspections and safety requirements tracked and handled, so nothing lapses quietly.

Questions

Common questions

Do you manage buildings you don't own?+

Yes — selectively. Multifamily and mixed-use buildings in the markets we already operate in, where our team and vendors are on the ground.

What does it cost?+

Priced per building after a walkthrough — size, condition and scope drive it. You'll get the number in writing before anything is signed.

Can you take over a building mid-stream?+

Yes. We start with a walkthrough and a records review — leases, rent roll, open work — then a written plan for the first 90 days.
