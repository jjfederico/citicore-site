Process

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Start with a conversation

    Use the contact button below. Tell John what you're looking for — income, growth, timeline — and what questions you have. No obligation either way.

  2. Step 2

    See the actual deal

    When a project fits, you get the real underwriting: purchase price, renovation or construction budget, rents, and how the money comes back. The same numbers we use ourselves.

  3. Step 3

    Invest, project by project

    Each project is structured on its own, with the terms in writing before any money moves. You choose deal by deal — nothing is automatic.

  4. Step 4

    Stay close to it

    Regular updates as the project runs — construction progress, leasing, financials — and a direct line to John the whole way through.

Questions

Common questions

How do I hear about projects?+

Start with the contact button below. Once John knows what you're looking for, fitting projects come to you directly as they're underwritten — before they're spoken for.

What does a typical investment look like?+

It varies by project — the size, structure and timeline of a multifamily hold are different from a ground-up build. Every opportunity is presented with its own numbers and terms in writing, and the conversation with John is where the specifics get covered.

Do you invest your own money too?+

Yes. Citicore puts its own capital into every project it offers investors. If a deal isn't good enough for our money, it isn't offered for yours.

How will I know how my investment is doing?+

With regular project updates — progress, leasing and financials — and a standing direct line to John. These are local projects; you're welcome to drive by them.
