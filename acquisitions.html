Acquisition Criteria

What we buy

These are guidelines, not hard limits. If a property doesn't fit neatly, send it anyway.

Asset types
Multifamily from two-family homes up to larger apartment buildings. Also mixed-use and small commercial.
Geography
Massachusetts and southern New Hampshire, with a focus on Boston, the North Shore and Cape Cod. Good deals elsewhere are always considered.
Condition
Well-kept buildings and ones that need updating. Condition affects price, not whether we'll look.
Occupancy
Occupied or vacant. We keep existing leases in place and treat residents with respect.
Hold
Long-term ownership with in-house management. We are not assembling properties to flip.
Sources
Direct from owners, through brokers, and from estates, attorneys and lenders. Off-market preferred, listed deals welcome.

Process

How a deal works

  1. Step 1

    Send the basics

    An address and a way to reach you is enough to start. Share more when you're ready.

  2. Step 2

    We review and call

    We look at the numbers and the neighborhood, then call you. If it isn't a fit, we tell you plainly.

  3. Step 3

    Written offer

    After a walkthrough, we put clear terms in writing. We don't make it contingent on things we could have checked first.

  4. Step 4

    Close

    Attorneys handle the closing, and we work around your timeline. We hold to the price we agreed.

Helpful, not required

Questions

Common questions

Is my information kept confidential?+

Yes. Submissions go only to our acquisitions team. We don't share them and we don't contact tenants without your approval.

Do you buy buildings with tenants in place?+

Yes. Most of what we buy is occupied. Existing leases carry over and our in-house team takes over management at closing.

Do I need a broker to sell to you?+

No. Many owners come to us directly. If you already have a broker, we're glad to work through them.

What if my property is outside your main markets?+

Send it. Boston, the North Shore and Cape Cod are where we're most active, but we look at good buildings wherever they are.

Can the closing date work around my plans?+

Usually, yes. Tell us what you need, whether that's a quick close or more time, and we'll build the offer around it.
