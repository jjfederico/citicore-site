What we collect

When you submit a form on this site, we receive what you send us: your name, email, phone number, and the property or investment details you include. We also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies and collects standard usage data (pages visited, approximate location, device type).

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry and evaluate the property or opportunity you contacted us about. Analytics data is used only to understand how the site is used and improve it.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, and we do not add form submissions to marketing lists. Information is shared only with the service providers that deliver our email and host this site, as needed for them to do that work.
